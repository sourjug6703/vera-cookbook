## Boundary cautions and resolved source decisions

- Cross-reference-only recipes with no printed ingredient list use an empty
  outer ingredient-section array; do not invent ingredient lines to satisfy
  the schema.
- Stuffed turkey owns its four stuffing sections. Stuffed pigeon owns its
  `Stuffing:` section. Salmon trout owns its `Sauce:` section.
- Yeast dumplings spans PDF 40–41; Tripe soup spans 43–44; Czech doughnuts spans
  53–54; Christmas loaf spans 57–58.
- The five fillings on PDFs 50–51 and Sugar icing, Chocolate filling, and Mocha
  filling are separately indexed component records.
- PDFs 67–68 are one composite fritter family with shared batter and named
  variant/accompaniment subsections, not five top-level records.
- `Christmas cookies` is a group heading, not a complete recipe.
- `vera-r0001`, `vera-r0011`, and `vera-r0077`: the owner classified the
  outlined-arrow ornaments as non-text decoration; their wording is excluded
  from transcription while the visual evidence remains.
- `vera-r0025`: the owner confirmed the blue handwritten marginal word as
  `Curry`.
- `vera-r0044`–`vera-r0046`: the owner classified the pale overlapping
  PDF-page-32 marks as non-text artifacts; the source-clear printed wording is
  retained.
- `vera-r0110`: the owner classified the handwritten `2 1/2` annotation as an
  authoritative correction to the printed flour quantity.
