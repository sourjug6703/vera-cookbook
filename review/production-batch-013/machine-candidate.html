Apple pancakes · Jablkové palačinky

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1·1/2 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 5 eggs, separated
  • pinch of salt
  • 2 large apples, Granny Smith type, peeled, diced
  • 6 tablespoons butter for frying
  • 2 tablespoons sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. Cream egg yolks and sugar, using wire whisk or electric mixer at low speed. When mixture becomes foamy, gradually add flour, salt and milk mixing well after each addition.
  2. Blend in apples and stiffly whipped egg whites. Drop by scoopfuls in hot butter and fry on both sides. Roll each pancake, sprinkle with sugar and place on a serving dish.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 45 minutes

God’s graces · Boží milosti

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla sugar
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • dash of salt
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 3 tablespoons white wine
  • 1 cup vegetable oil or butter for frying
  • 1/3 cup sugar mixed with vanilla sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. On a lightly floured surface sift together flour, salt, sugar and vanilla sugar. Make a well in the center. Put egg yolks, cream, wine and crumbled butter in the well. Knead, with the palms of your hands, until dough is smooth and shiny. Roll out into a paper-thin sheet. Cut into 2" squares. Make two 1" cuts inside each square. Fry in hot oil or butter on both sides, until lightly golden. Drain on paper towel, sprinkle with sugar mixed with vanilla sugar.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Pretzels, sweet · Preclíčky

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 cups flour
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 8 tablespoons butter, creamed
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 2 oz. almonds, grated or crushed
  • 3 hard boiled egg yolks, puréed
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • grease for cookie sheet
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • crystal sugar

Instructions

  1. On a floured board sift together flour and sugar, make a well in center. Thoroughly blend all remaining ingredients and place in well. Gradually mixing in flour, knead with the palms of your hands until smooth. Form a neat ball. Let dough rest at least 1 hour in a cool place, or refrigerate overnight. Divide dough into equal pieces, the size of a ping-pong ball. Form pencil-thin strands and shape them into pretzels. Place on a greased cookie sheet, brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with crystal sugar. Bake at 350F 10-12 minutes or until golden.

Preparation time: 2 hours

Nut cake · Ořechový dort

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs, separated
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup hazelnuts or walnuts, ground or finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • butter and flour for cake pan

Instructions

  1. Beat egg whites until stiff, set aside. In a separate bowl cream sugar and egg yolks, adding one at a time and beating well after each addition. Add 1 tablespoon water. Continue beating until light and fluffy, about 10 minutes total. Fold in flour, nuts and stiffly whipped egg whites. Mix thoroughly, pour batter into buttered and floured 9" spring form cake pan. Preheat oven to 350F, bake about 40 minutes or until cake tester or wooden pick comes out clean and cake separates from side of spring form.
  2. When cooled, cut cake horizontally, spread evenly with chocolate or mocca filling, then frost top and sides with the same. Decorate with walnuts or pecan halves or tosted almonds slivers.

Yield / time

1 cake – Preparation time: 1 hour

Chocolate filling · Čokoládový krém

Ingredients

  • 1 pint heavy cream
  • 1·1/2 cups semi sweet chocolate morsels

Instructions

  1. Combine cream and chocolate in a saucepan, set over low heat and bring to boil, stirring constantly. Remove from heat and pour mixture in a clean bowl. Refrigerate at least 3 hours or overnight. Whip with electric mixer at medium speed until smooth and creamy for about 7 minutes.

Mocha filling · Mocca krém

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 14 tablespoons butter
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 3 tablespoons strong black coffee

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan cream sugar and egg yolks. Add coffee, set over medium heat. Stir constantly until mixture becomes hot. Do not boil. Remove from heat, turn mixture into a clean bowl, let cool. In a bowl cream butter and gradually add the cooled sugar/egg yolks/coffee mixture. Beat until fluffy.

Vanilla horns · Vanilkové rohlíčky

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 8 oz. ground almonds
  • 3 sticks butter
  • 2 packages vanilla sugar (1 package into dough, 1 for sprinkling)
  • 4 tablespoons sugar mixed with vanilla sugar

Instructions

  1. On a floured pastry board knead all ingredients into a ball. Use palms of your hands, not fingers, so that dough will not crumble. Dough is ready when smooth and no longer sticks to the board. Refrigerate 1 hour, or overnight as it will be easier to work with. Shape tiny horns the size of your little finger or smaller, according to your preference and patience. A small grinder is available in some speciality stores, which makes them all equal in size and is faster than the "manual method". Place horns on a clean dry cookie sheet, 1" apart. Preheat oven to 325F, bake 15-20 minutes, until pale golden. Remove from oven and let stand 5 minutes. While hot, sprinkle with sugar mixed with vanilla sugar. When cooled remove carefully from cookie sheet. They keep for many weeks in an airtight tin.

Yield / time

Preparation time: 1 hour 15 minutes, plus refrigeration

Almond snow-balls · Kostrby

Ingredients

  • 3 egg whites
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 5 oz. slivered almonds
  • Butter and flour for cookie sheet

Instructions

  1. Beat egg whites and sugar in a bain-marie, until the mixture thickens. Remove from heat, stir in almonds. Drop by teaspoonfuls on a buttered and floured cookie sheet 1\frac{1}{2} " apart. Bake 14 minutes at 325F. Snow-balls should puff-up and be pale-golden. Remove carefully from cookie sheet.
  2. Let snow-balls dry thoroughly, at least 2 hours, in a warm oven under 200F.
  3. Store in a dry place.

Butter rounds · Máslová kolečka

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/3 cup sugar mixed with 1 teaspoon vanilla sugar
  • 1 stick butter
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • red raspberry or red currant preserve

Instructions

  1. On a floured pastry board knead all ingredients into a ball using the palms of your hands, not the fingers. The dough will be smooth and will not crumble. You can let it rest for a few hours, overnight or more. You can use it right away also.
  2. With a roller pin roll the dough out as thin as possible. (1/8"). Cut it into 1-1/4" round cakes, using a cookie cutter or a glass. Separate half of the round cakes and cut out a small round hole in the center, the size of a thimble. Use a cookie cutter or a thimble. Place the round cakes on a clean, dry cookie sheet and bake at 350F for 15 minutes, or until they are pink/golden. Remove from oven and cool. Before serving put 1/2 teaspoon of red raspberry or red currant seedless preserve in the center of each plain round, top it with one "holed" round. The red color is important for the look of the cookies and the sour flavor of raspberry or currant compliments them well. Unfilled cookies can be stored for weeks in an airtight container.

Preparation time - 1 hour 30 minutes

Truffles · Lanýže

Ingredients

  • 2/3 cup heavy cream
  • 6 oz. ground walnuts or hazelnuts
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla sugar
  • 1 egg white
  • 3/4 cup grated dark semi-sweet chocolate

Instructions

  1. Pour the cream in a saucepan and set over medium heat. When hot, but not boiling, add sugar, vanilla sugar and the ground nuts. Stirring steadily, boil until the mixture stiffens and separates from the pan. Remove from heat. Let it cool or refrigerate overnight. Next day: Make little balls, dip each in egg white and dredge in grated chocolate. Let the "truffles" dry for 10 minutes and place them in petit-four holders.

Yield / time

Preparation time: 30 minutes, plus refrigeration.
