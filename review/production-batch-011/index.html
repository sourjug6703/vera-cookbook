Karlsbader biscuits · Karlovarské suchary

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 lbs. flour
  • 1-3/4 cups milk
  • 1-1/2 oz. fresh yeast
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 10 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla sugar
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 10 oz. chopped almonds
  • grease for baking pan
  • 1 cup sugar mixed with vanilla sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. Leavening: in a bowl stir yeast with 2 teaspoons sugar until liquefied. Add 3/4 cup flour and 3/4 cup lukewarm milk. Mix thoroughly, knead, form a ball. Dust lightly with flour, cover with a napkin. Let rise 30 minutes, or until doubled in bulk.
  2. Place all remaining ingredients and the risen leavening on a floured pastry board, knead with the palms of your hands, until dough feels smooth, dry and elastic. Sprinkle with flour, cover with napkin, let rise 40 minutes.
  3. Punch dough down, shape into a loaf and transfer into a buttered and floured loaf pan. Let rise 15 minutes. Preheat oven to 350F, bake 30 minutes, or until top is golden.
  4. Next day: Cut loaf into 1/3" thick slices. Dredge each slice in sugar mixed with vanilla sugar, shake off excess. Put them on a clean dry baking sheet, brown lightly on both sides in 250-300F oven.
  5. Biscuits keep fresh for many weeks in an airtight tin.

Yield / time

Preparation time: 2 hours 30 minutes, plus 30 minutes next day

Easter bread · Bochánek

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 8 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 whole egg
  • grated rind of one lemon
  • dash of salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup golden raisins
  • 1/2 cup slivered almonds
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg for brushing, butter for baking tray

Instructions

  1. Leavening: In a bowl stir the yeast and 2 teaspoons sugar until the yeast dissolves. Add 1/2 cup flour and one third of the milk and knead it into a ball. Sprinkle with flour and set aside to rise, in a warm, draft free area. It will double in bulk within 20 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl combine the remaining flour, sugar, salt and lemon rind. Make a well in the center and place the risen leavening, egg yolks and whole egg in it. Gradually adding the remaining milk, mix well. Lastly add raisins and melted butter and knead with a wooden spoon, or with hands, until dough is smooth and no longer sticks to the bowl. Set aside, dust with flour and let rise 30 minutes. Punch dough down, let rise 30 minutes again.
  3. After the dough has risen at the second time, turn it over a floured pastry board and divide in two halves. Shape each into a ball. Place on a baking tray lined with buttered wax paper. Let rise 30 minutes. Brush with lightly beaten egg. With a sharp knife slit the top of each bread cross-wise. Let rise 10 minutes. Preheat oven to 350°F. Sprinkle with slivered almonds and bake 30-35 minutes or until breads are golden brown. Dust with sugar and cool at room temperature.

Yield / time

2 breads – Preparation time: 3 hours 30 minutes

Christmas loaf · Vánočka

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup golden raisins
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • dash of salt
  • 1 beaten egg for brushing
  • 3 tablespoons sugar mixed with vanilla sugar for dusting
  • 1/3 cup slivered almonds to sprinkle over loaf before baking

Instructions

  1. Leavening: In a bowl stir yeast and 2 teaspoons sugar until dissolved. Add 1/2 cup flour and 1/3 cup lukewarm milk. Knead into a ball, dust with flour, let rise 20-30 minutes, until doubled in bulk.
  2. In a separate bowl combine remaining flour, sugar, salt, lemon rind. Make a well in center. Put egg yolks and risen leavening in the well. Gradually adding remaining lukewarm milk, mix thoroughly. Add raisins and melted butter, knead dough until smooth, dry and elastic. Dust with flour, let rise 30 minutes.
  3. Punch dough down, let rise again 30 minutes.
  4. Turn dough over onto a lightly floured pastry board, divide in two halves.
  5. Divide one half into 3 identical sections. Form 3 ropes 13" long. Braid them, pinch together both ends. Place on oven tray lined with buttered wax paper. Make a shallow indentation in center lengthwise,
  6. Optional:
  7. then brush depression lightly with a beaten egg white. Divide remaining dough into 5 identical sections. Form 5 ropes 13" long. Braid 3 ropes and place on top of the thicker braid. Again make indentation in center and brush with beaten egg white. Braid last two ropes, place on top of second layer. Press each end firmly together. Let loaf rise 30 minutes. Brush with beaten egg.
  8. Optional: sprinkle with slivered almonds.
  9. Preheat oven to 375F. Bake 10 minutes. Reduce oven temperature to 350F, bake 20-25 minutes, check with wooden pick for doneness. Dust with sugar mixed with vanilla sugar while hot. Cool at room temperature at least 1 hour. Slice.

Bishop’s bread (fruit cake) · Biskupský chlebíček

Ingredients

  • 1-1/4 cups flour
  • 1 whole egg
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 cup diced dried apricots, other dry fruit can substitute
  • 1/4 cup slivered almonds
  • 1/2 cup golden raisins
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • grated rind of one lemon

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan combine the egg yolks, the egg, sugar and lemon rind and set over medium/low heat. Beat until the mixture is lukewarm. Remove from heat and continue beating until cool and creamy. Fold in the flour mixed with baking powder, dried apricots, almonds, raisins. Lastly, add melted butter. Turn the batter into a buttered oblong loaf pan lined with wax paper. Bake 30 minutes at 350F. Test for doneness with a cake tester or toothpick. Inserting it in the center, it should come out clean. Let stand for 5 minutes before turning the bread out onto a cake rack. Dust lightly with sugar. Cool and slice.
  2. Optional: Spread apricot jelly very thinly over the bread and top with sugar icing. When cooled, or when the icing hardens (approximately 2 hours), slice.

Yield / time

1 bread – Preparation time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Cherry cake · Bublanina

Ingredients

  • 2·1/2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 10 tablespoons butter
  • 2 eggs, separated
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla sugar
  • 2·1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 1 lb. cherries (preferably Morello cherries)
  • butter or margarine and flour for baking dish
  • sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. Beat two egg whites and half of the sugar until stiff, set aside. In a bowl combine the butter, the egg yolks and the remaining sugar and beat until creamy and fluffy. Add lemon rind, vanilla sugar and milk, mix well. Fold in the beaten egg whites by spoonfuls, mixing gently after each addition. Fold in the flour mixed with baking powder. Pour the batter into a buttered and floured baking dish – fill no more than half full. Spread the cherries evenly over the batter and bake at 375F for 30 minutes, or until golden on top. Remove from oven, cool, cut into squares, dust with sugar.

Yield / time

Serves 8 – Preparation time: 45 minutes
