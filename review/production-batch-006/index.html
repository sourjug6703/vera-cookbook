Stuffed turkey · Krocan nadívaný

Ingredients

Giblets stuffing:
  • Giblets from the turkey
  • 3 hard baked rolls
  • 1 cup milk or water
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs
  • salt, marjoram, thyme
Chestnuts stuffing:
  • 1 lb. chestnuts
  • 10 tablespoons butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 5 hard baked rolls
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs
  • dash of ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar
  • salt
Almond stuffing:
  • 5 oz. chopped or sliced almonds
  • 8 tablespoons butter
  • 4 egg yolks, 3 egg whites
  • 5 hard baked rolls
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup bread crumbs
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Prepare the bird as indicated in “roast turkey”. Turn the neck into the body cavity to block the passage between the neck and the body. The stuffing will stay in the neck cavity while roasting. Lightly fill neck cavity with stuffing. Do not pack · stuffing will expand while cooking. Fasten neck skin with stitches or skewer. Roast turkey as shown above.
Giblets stuffing:
  1. Soak the rolls in milk or water, squeeze off excess liquid, shred them. Rinse and finely chop the giblets.
  2. In a bowl cream butter, eggs, add the chopped onion, thyme, marjoram, shredded rolls, giblets, bread crumbs. Salt the mixture and mix thoroughly.
Chestnuts stuffing:
  1. Simmer chestnuts in water for 10·15 minutes. When the shells crack, peel and chop them coarsely.
  2. Break the rolls, soak them in milk, squeeze off excess, shred them. In a bowl cream butter, one whole egg and three egg yolks. Add the shredded rolls, nutmeg, sugar and coarsely chopped chestnuts. Salt the mixture; Fold in half of the bread crumbs and three stiffly whipped egg whites.
  3. Turn the stuffing into a fluted tube pan or a flan ring, buttered and dusted with bread crumbs. Bake in a bain-marie at 350F for 1·1/2 hours. When done, let it stand 5 minutes, then invert on a serving dish. Slice it like a cake.
  4. Serve with apricot or pear compote, cranberries, etc.
Almond stuffing:
  1. Break the rolls and soak them in milk. Squeeze off excess, shred and strain them.
  2. In a bowl cream butter, egg yolks and sugar. Combine all ingredients. Fold in three stiffly beaten egg whites.
  3. Bake and serve in the same manner as chestnut stuffing.
Raisins stuffing:
  1. Same as almond stuffing, replacing half of the almonds with 3/4 cup golden raisins.

Yield / time

Preparation time: 30 minutes

Preparation time: 2 hours

Preparation time: 2 hours

Notes

  • (Have chestnuts ready ahead of time. Bake stuffing while roasting turkey, so that both will be done at the same time).

Stuffed pigeon · Holub nadívaný

Ingredients

  • 3 young pigeons
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • salt
  • 3/4 cup chicken stock
Stuffing:
  • 4 and 2 tablespoons butter (2. tbsp for braising the liver)
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 egg white
  • 2 hard baked rolls
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • liver from three pigeons, chopped
  • parsley
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs
  • salt

Instructions

Prepare stuffing first:
  1. Soak the rolls in milk, squeeze off excess, shred them. Cream the butter and the egg yolks, add the shredded rolls, the liver sautéed in butter, salt, snipped parsley, bread crumbs. Add stiffly whipped egg white.
  2. Carefully loosen the skin of the breasts and fill the pockets with stuffing. Enclose with stitches.
  3. Salt the pigeons. Place them in a roasting pan with melted butter, breasts down, roast at 350F for 20 minutes.
  4. Baste with melted butter, juices from the pan and a little chicken stock.
  5. Turn pigeons breasts up and finish roasting in the same manner.
  6. To serve, cut the pigeons in halves, sprinkle with pan drippings and snipped parsley. (Remove the stitches, of course).
  7. Serve with potatoes, green peas, salad, etc.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Roast goose · Husa pečená

Ingredients

  • 1 goose, 10 lbs.
  • 4·5 medium apples
  • salt, caraway seeds
  • water

Instructions

  1. Rinse goose, pat dry. Rinse the apples-do not peel them. Rub the goose inside and out with salt and caraway seeds. Fill body cavity with apples.
  2. Place goose in a roasting pan with a little hot water at the bottom.
  3. Roast at 350F, first breast side down for 1·1/2 hrs, basting with water and pan drippings. When golden brown on top, turn goose breast side up. Continue roasting for 1-1/2 hours. While roasting, prick the skin with a fork over the fatty areas to release fat. (Just the skin, not the meat. If the juice from the meat runs out, the goose will become dry.) Total roasting time is approximately 20 minutes per pound. During the last 15 minutes of roasting, raise oven temperature to 400F and stop basting to achieve a crunchy skin. Remove apples and slice goose. Skim fat from pan juices and pour into a gravy boat to accompany goose. Serve with dumplings, potato dumplings, cabbage, red cabbage, sauerkraut.

Yield / time

Serves 8-10 – Preparation time: 3 hours 30 minutes

Duckling with Madeira wine · Kachna na víně

Ingredients

  • 1 duckling, 5-6 lbs.
  • 5 slices ham, cut in stripes
  • 8 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup Madeira wine
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Rinse duckling, pat dry. Sprinkle with salt and pepper inside and out. Melt butter in a roasting pan, add ham and duckling. Brown it quickly on both sides under the broiler. Lower oven temperature to 350F. Pour Madeira wine over duckling. Add some of the chicken stock, salt and pepper, and cover with buttered waxpaper. Roast 1 to 1·1/2 hours, occasionally adding the remaining chicken stock.
  2. Transfer duckling to a heated platter. Skim fat from pan drippings and strain into a saucepan. Add flour and slowly cook over low heat until the mixture thickens.
  3. Quarter duckling, serve with the sauce, boiled potatoes, vegetables.

Yield / time

Serves 4 – Preparation time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Rabbit with paprika sauce · Králík na paprice

Ingredients

  • 1 rabbit, 4·5 lbs., skinned, cleaned, cut in pieces
  • 3 slices bacon, cut in strips
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tablespoons paprika
  • 6 grains each of whole allspice and black peppercorns
  • thyme, 2 bay leaves
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1·1/2 cups sour cream
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Rinse, pat dry rabbit pieces. Sprinkle with salt and lard each with bacon strips.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan; add onion and cook until translucent. Add paprika, whole allspice, black peppercorns, thyme, bay leaves and rabbit pieces.
  3. Simmer, covered, for 1·1/2 hours, or until rabbit is tender, adding chicken stock when needed.
  4. Using a slotted spoon, lift rabbit pieces from liquid and place in a casserole.
  5. Stir flour into the liquid and simmer uncovered for 5 minutes. Add remaining chicken stock and sour cream and cook, stirring as necessary, until sauce is reduced to desired consistency, about 5 minutes. Strain sauce over rabbit. Simmer until heated through.
  6. Serve with gnocchi, dumplings, noodles.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 2 hours

Fried rabbit · Králík smažený

Ingredients

  • 1 rabbit, 4·5 lbs., skinned, cleaned, cut in small serving portions
  • 2 eggs, whole
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1·1/2 cups bread crumbs
  • 1 cup vegetable oil for frying
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Scald rabbit with hot water, pat dry. Pound each piece lightly, sprinkle with salt. Dredge each in flour shaking off excess, dip in mixture of beaten eggs and milk, coat with bread crumbs. Hare in cream sauce · Zajíc na smetaně

Ingredients

  • Hindquarters of 1 hare, 4·5 lbs., cleaned, membrane removed
  • 3 slices bacon cut in short strips
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • carrot, celery, parsnip – sliced
  • 5 grains each of black peppercorn, whole allspice, juniper berries
  • thyme, bay leaf, ground nutmeg
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 1·1/2 cups beef stock
  • 1·1/2 cups heavy cream
  • juice of 1·2 lemons
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Rinse the hare, pat dry. Rub with crushed juniper berries, sprinkle with salt, lard with bacon strips.
  2. Melt butter in a heavy skillet, add onion and vegetables, and sauté over medium heat.
  3. When onion is translucent, add hare and brown evenly on all sides.
  4. Transfer all into a roaster. Add spices and some of the beef stock.
  5. Roast 1 hour at 350F larded side down, then turn hare larded side up and continue roasting until tender, about 1 hour. Baste frequently with pan juices and beef stock.
  6. Remove hare to carving board.
  7. Make sauce by combining pan juices with flour. Simmer 5 minutes, stirring constantly. Add juice of 1 lemon and cream. Simmer a little more. Season to taste with salt, pepper and more lemon juice, if needed. Strain.
  8. To serve, slice hare and place slices on a warmed platter. Pour sauce over top.
  9. Serve remaining sauce in a gravy boat.
  10. Serve with rice, noodles, vegetables.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 2 hours 30 minutes

Marinated hare in wine sauce · Zajíc na černo

Ingredients

Marinade:
  • 1 cup sliced carrots
  • 1 cup sliced onions
  • 1 cup sliced shallots
  • 3 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 10 black peppercorns
  • 3 bay leaves
  • thyme
  • 1/4 cup cognac
  • 3 cups red wine
  • 1·1/2 cups vegetable oil
Hare and sauce:
  • Front part of 1 hare, 4·5 lbs.
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 3 oz. bacon, diced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons vinegar
  • 1·1/2 cups beef stock
  • 1 cup red wine
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 5 grains each of black peppercorns and whole allspice
  • 2 bay leaves, thyme
  • 4 tablespoons flour
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup cranberry sauce
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

To marinate:
  1. Place hare in a large crock. Add all dry ingredients, top with cognac, add wine.
  2. Add oil to seal air out. The hare must be submerged in the marinade. Soak hare overnight, or even better 2·3 days.
  3. Remove and wipe dry with a cloth.
  4. Heat butter in a 4 quart top·of·range casserole. Add bacon and onion and cook until soft. Add all spices and lemon rind. Sprinkle hare with salt and vinegar, put into casserole, and brown lightly on all sides.
  5. Reduce heat. Add some of the beef stock.
  6. Cover tightly and simmer 1·1·1/2 hours, or until meat is tender when pierced with a fork. Add beef stock occasionally.
  7. Transfer hare to a heated platter.
  8. Skim off fat from the cooking liquid, stir in the flour and continue cooking to a rich brown. Add the remaining beef stock and the wine.
  9. Cook until sauce thickens. Strain, blend in sugar and cranberry sauce. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Bring to boil, stirring constantly.
  10. Slice hare, pour hot sauce over top.
  11. Serve with noodles, gnocchi, dumplings.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 2 hours,plus marinating
