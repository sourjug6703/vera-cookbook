Fresh ham in cream sauce · Vepřová kýta na smetaně

Ingredients

  • 3·4 lbs. fresh ham, boned and skinned
  • 4 slices bacon
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 celery root
  • 1 parsnip
  • 3 bay leaves, thyme, whole allspice, whole black pepper
  • 1 lemon
  • 1·1/2 cups sour cream
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1·1/2 cups chicken stock or water
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Dice the carrots, celery root and parsnip. Cut the bacon into short strips.
  2. Skim off fat from the meat, rinse and pat it dry, salt it and lard with bacon strips.
  3. In a large stewpot combine melted butter, vegetables, spices, lemon rind, grated or chopped.
  4. Set over medium heat. Add the meat and brown it lightly on all sides. Lower the heat, cover tightly and simmer over low heat until tender, about 2 hours, gradually adding the chicken stock or water.
  5. Remove meat from pot, keep it warm.
  6. Strain the gravy into a saucepan. Combine flour and sour cream and blend it into the gravy. Add lemon juice, simmer 5 minutes.
  7. Slice meat, pour the gravy over it or serve separately.
  8. Serve with dumplings, noodles, vegetables.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 3 hours

Pork chops with caraway seeds · Vepřová žebírka na kmíně

Ingredients

  • 6 pork chops
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 teaspoons caraway seeds
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Pound the pork chops lightly·they should not be more than 1/2" thick. Make a few short cuts on the edges to prevent curling, salt them on both sides and dust with flour on one side. Brown chops in hot oil, floured side first.
  2. In a large skillet combine the butter and finely chopped onion and set over medium heat.
  3. When the onion becomes translucent, add caraway seeds and some of the chicken stock. Add the pork chops and simmer about 30 minutes or until they are tender.
  4. Remove the chops, bring the liquid to boil, blend in flour and chicken stock and simmer until the sauce thickens. The sauce should be medium thick. Strain.
  5. Return pork chops in the sauce, simmer until heated through.
  6. Serve with boiled potatoes, rice, salad.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 45 minutes

Breaded pork chops · Vepřová žebírka smažená

Ingredients

  • 6 pork loin rib chops, cut about 1/2" thick
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup bread crumbs
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Pound the pork chops lightly; make a few short cuts on the edges to prevent curling.
  2. Spice them with salt and pepper. Dredge them in flour shaking off excess, dip in beaten eggs and coat with bread crumbs.
  3. Fry the chops in a covered pan for 5 minutes, turn them and finish frying uncovered. Drain on paper towel.
  4. Serve with potato salad, green salad, vegetables.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 45 minutes

Pork goulash · Guláš vepřový

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs. boned pork shoulder, cut in 1·1/2" cubes
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons paprika
  • 1 teaspoon caraway seeds
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 4 tablespoons flour
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion in oil. Add paprika, vinegar, pork, caraway seeds, pepper, few spoons of beef stock. Cover and simmer 1·1/2 hrs, or until meat is tender. Stir occasionally and gradually add beef stock, little at a time, just enough to keep moisture in the stew.
  2. Blend flour, sour cream and 1/2 cup beef stock. Stir the mixture in the pan with the meat. Simmer 10·15 minutes. If the sauce is too thick, add more beef stock.
  3. Taste for salt. Should you prefer to redden the color of the sauce, add 2·3 tablespoons tomato paste.
  4. Serve with potatoes or dumplings.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 2 hours
