Potato apple pie · Jablkový koláč bramborový

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 medium potatoes, boiled, mashed
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • grease for baking dish
Filling:
  • 1/2 cup hazelnuts or walnuts, ground or chopped
  • 3 medium apples, Granny Smith, peeled, thinly sliced parboiled
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • rind of 1 lemon

Instructions

  1. Blend baking powder into flour. Add all ingredients and mix thoroughly. Knead about 10 minutes, until dough is smooth and dry. On a floured surface roll dough out to 1/3" thickness, cut in two halves. Place one half in a buttered baking dish, evenly spread apple mixture. Cover with other half of dough. Preheat oven to 375F, bake 30 minutes or until golden on top. Cut in squares, lightly dust with sugar.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Bread and apple baba · Žemlovka

Ingredients

  • 6 hard baked rolls, italian or french, sliced 1/3" thick
  • 1 quart milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • dash of salt
  • 3 large apples, Granny Smith type, peeled, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup golden raisins
  • 2 tablespoons sugar mixed with 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 4 tablespoons butter

Instructions

  1. Beat lightly milk, eggs, sugar, salt. Soak bread slices in the mixture. Squeeze off excess and arrange bread in a single layer in a generously buttered ovenproof casserole. Follow with a layer of apples. Spread raisins and cinnamon/sugar evenly over apples. Top with a layer of bread slices. Preheat oven to 350F. Bake 30-40 minutes until top is golden brown. Dust with sugar. Serve hot or cooled.

Yield / time

Serves 10 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Apple strudel · Jablkový závin

Ingredients

Dough:
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 whole egg
  • dash of salt
Filling:
  • 6 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup bread crumbs
  • 3 large apples, Granny Smith
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup golden raisins
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Place the flour on a pastry board and make a well in the center. Pour lukewarm water in the well. Add salt, softened butter and the egg. With the palm of your hands knead all ingredients into a ball. Let it rest in a warm place for 30 minutes.
  2. Turn the dough over a floured cloth napkin, flatten it a little with a rolling pin and brush with melted hot butter. Then stretch it with hands into a thin oblong shape. Cover two thirds with bread crumbs browned in butter.
  3. Peel the apples, slice them thinly and spread them evenly over the bread crumbs. Top with sugar mixed with cinnamon and raisins. Brush the edges of the dough with melted butter. With the help of the napkin roll the dough with the filling into a loaf. Put the loaf on a greased baking sheet and brush it with butter. Bake 30 minutes at 375F. When done, slice it and dust with sugar. May be served warm, or cooled. Can be reheated.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Yellow cake · Bábovka

Ingredients

  • 2-1/2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 10 tablespoons butter
  • 1 whole egg, 3 egg yolks, 4 egg whites
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla sugar
  • 1/2 cup golden raisins
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • butter and flour for baking pan
  • sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. In a bowl cream the butter, sugar, whole egg, egg yolks, lemon rind and vanilla sugar. Beat at medium speed until fluffy. Add the milk, raisins and half of the flour, mix thoroughly. In the end fold in stiffly whipped egg whites and the remaining flour mixed with baking powder. Pour the batter in a buttered and floured fluted tube pan and bake for one hour at 350F. Test for doneness with a tester or toothpick. Turn the cake out onto a cake rack, dust lightly with powdered sugar, cool and slice.

Fudge Marble cake · Mramorová bábovka

Ingredients

  • 2-1/4 cups flour
  • 1-1/3 cups sugar
  • 10 tablespoons butter
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 3 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 3 tablespoons water
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla sugar
  • 1-1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • butter and flour for baking pan
  • sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. In a bowl cream the butter, sugar, egg yolks, vanilla sugar and lemon rind. Beat until fluffy. Add the milk and the flour mixed with baking powder. Fold in the stiffly whipped egg whites. Pour half of the batter in a separate bowl. Dissolve the cocoa powder in water and combine with one of the yellow batter halves. Turn the mixture into a buttered and floured fluted tube pan, alternating the yellow and the brown batter.
  2. Bake at 350F for 1 hour, or until the toothpick comes out clean. Invert the cake onto wire rack, dust with sugar, cool and slice.

Yield / time

1 cake – Preparation time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Plum dumplings · Švestkové knedlíky

Ingredients

  • 4·1/2 cups flour
  • 8 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 whole egg, 1 egg yolk
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 2 lbs. plums, apricots, cherries or other fruits
Topping:
  • 3/4 cup bread crumbs
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 4 tablespoons butter
Topping variations
  • (1) Sugar mixed with a little cinnamon (2) Ground poppy seeds mixed with sugar (3) Grated farmers cheese (4) Cream or sour cream can substitute the hot butter

Instructions

  1. In a bowl cream the butter, the egg, the egg yolk and sugar, using wire whisk or electric mixer at low speed. Add flour and lukewarm milk. Knead the dough until it no longer sticks to the bowl. Turn it over on a floured pastry board and roll it out to 1/2" thickness. Cut in squares 2 × 2". Place one plum, or other fruit pitted or not, in the center of each square. Enclose the dumplings by pressing the four corners firmly together and round them in the palm of your hand. Boil in lightly salted water for 6 - 7 minutes. Drain. Sprinkle with browned bread crumbs, sugar and melted hot butter.
  2. These dumplings can be prepared ahead of time and reheated in double boiler.
  3. Serve warm as a dessert or a meal.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Apple, plum, peach, banana fritters (or other fruits of similar consistency) · Ovocné kobližky

Ingredients

Batter for frying (1):
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 egg, whole
  • 1 teaspoon rum
Batter for frying (2):
  • 2/3 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup white wine
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 eggs, separated
  • 1 teaspoon vegetable oil
  • pinch of salt
Apple fritters - Jablkové kobližky
  • 4-5 apples
  • 1-1/2 cups oil or butter for frying
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • batter for frying
Apricot sauce - Meruňková omáčka
  • 1 cup apricot preserve
  • 1 cup white wine
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon water.
Plum or peach fritters - Švestkové, broskvové kobližky
  • 2 lbs. plums or peaches
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 1-1/2 cups oil or butter for frying
  • batter for frying

Instructions

  1. In a bowl blend flour, salt, milk, mixing constantly (use wire whisk or electric mixer at low speed). Add egg, rum and sugar. Mix well.
  2. In a bowl thoroughly blend egg yolks and wine. Gradually add flour, salt, sugar and oil. (use wire whisk or electric mixer at low speed). Gently fold in stiffly whipped egg whites.
  3. Peel and core the apples. Cut them in round slices (approx 1/3" thick), dip each in the batter and deep fry until golden brown. Drain on a paper towel, dust with sugar and serve. Or serve with apricot sauce.
  4. In a saucepan combine the wine, lemon rind, apricot preserve and sugar. Set over medium heat and bring to boil stirring constantly. Boil for 1 minute. Add the cornstarch dissolved in water and boil one minute more. Pass the sauce through a sieve. Serve warm with warm desserts and cold with cold desserts.
  5. Peel and pit the plums or peaches. Cut in halves or sections, dip in the batter and deep fry until golden brown. Drain on paper towel, dust with sugar.
  6. Sliced bananas and other fruits may be prepared in the same manner.

Cabbage pockets · Zelné taštičky

Ingredients

Dough:
  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled, mashed, cooled
  • 1-1/2 cups flour
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • dash of salt
  • 1 egg white for brushing
Stuffing:
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • salt and pepper
Topping:
  • 3/4 cup bread crumbs
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons sugar

Instructions

  1. Make stuffing: Sauté onion in oil until translucent. Stir in sugar. Add cabbage, salt and pepper, cook 10-15 minutes, until cabbage is tender. Do not overcook. Properly cooked cabbage should be crisp.
  2. Combine dough ingredients and knead until smooth, for about 10 minutes.
  3. On a floured surface roll dough out to 1/4" thickness. Cut out 2-1/2" squares. Drop a spoonful of cabbage in center of each square. Brush edges with egg white, fold squares to form a triangle. Press edges firmly together.
  4. Boil slowly in lightly salted water for 5 minutes.
  5. Drain, dredge in bread crumbs browned in butter, sprinkle with sugar.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Country pancakes · Lívance

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1·1/3 cups milk
  • 1/2 oz. fresh yeast
  • 2 eggs, separated
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • dash of salt
  • grease for griddle Topping:
  • 2 tablespoons sugar mixed with 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 4 tablespoons melted butter Optional:
  • 1/2 cup each of fruit jam and sour cream

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl stir yeast and 2 teaspoons sugar until dissolved. Gradually add lukewarm milk, flour, salt, remaining sugar, nutmeg, and egg yolks. Mix thoroughly, let rise in a warm place 30 minutes or until doubled in bulk. Gently fold in stiffly whipped egg whites. Let rise 15 minutes.
  2. Lightly grease griddle, and set over medium heat. After 4·5 minutes griddle should be hot enough for baking. Pour batter onto griddle by scoopfuls, about 3·1/2" in diameter, leaving at least 1" between cakes. Turn pancakes as they become puffy and full of bubbles on top.
  3. Brush each with melted butter, sprinkle with sugar mixed with cinnamon.
  4. Optional: Spread fruit jam over pancakes and dot with sour cream. Serve hot.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour 15 minutes
