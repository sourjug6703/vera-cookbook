Ricotta filling · Tvarohová nádivka

Ingredients

  • 8 oz. ricotta
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 egg yolks
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 2-3 tablespoons sponge crumbs
  • 1 tablespoon rum
  • 3 tablespoons golden raisins

Instructions

  1. In a bowl cream the sugar and the egg yolks. Add ricotta, lemon rind, sponge crumbs, rum and raisins - mix well. The sponge crumbs are added to thicken the filling. You can add a little more or less, to achieve the right consistency. If the filling is not thick enough, is runny, it would run out while baking and jeopardize the good look of the finished product.

Yield / time

Makes approx. 1 - 1/2 cups - Preparation time: 20 minutes

Walnuts filling · Ořechová nádivka

Ingredients

  • 6 oz. ground or chopped walnuts
  • 2/3 cup maple syrup
  • 3/4 cup sponge crumbs
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla sugar
  • 2 tablespoons heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Pour the maple syrup in a saucepan and bring over medium heat. When hot, but not boiling, remove from heat, add the walnuts, vanilla sugar, lemon rind and heavy cream and as much of the sponge crumbs as needed to thicken the mixture. Mix well.

Yield / time

Makes approx. 1 - 1/2 cups - Preparation time: 30 minutes

Apple filling · Jablková nádivka

Ingredients

  • 3 large apples (Granny Smith)
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon rum
  • 3 oz. ground or chopped almonds
  • 1/2 cup sponge crumbs

Instructions

  1. Peel, core and quarter the apples. In a saucepan combine apples and water and simmer until soft. Purée the apples in a bowl, and mix in all remaining ingredients. To attain the desired thickness, add some of the sponge crumbs.

Yield / time

Makes approx. 1 - 1/2 cups - Preparation time: 30 minutes

  • 2 tablespoons golden raisins

“Silver dollars” (miniature plain muffins) · Dukátové buchtičky

Ingredients

  • 4 cups coarse flour, Gold Medal Wondra
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 1-1/4 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • dash of salt
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 4 tablespoons butter for baking pan and brushing

Instructions

  1. Prepare dough in the same manner as in Bohemian muffins. Roll it out to 1/3" thickness. Using a cookie cutter or a glass 1" in diameter cut out round cakes and lay them tightly together in a buttered baking pan. Brush sides and top with melted butter. Let rise 30 minutes in a warm place. Preheat oven to 350F, bake 20-25 minutes or until golden brown on top.
  2. Serve warm with vanilla sauce or wine froth.

Yield / time

Serves 10 – Preparation time: 2 hours 30 minutes.

Vanilla sauce - Vanilková omáčka

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk, or light cream
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla sugar
  • 1 tablespoon corn starch

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan cream egg yolks, sugar mixed with vanilla sugar, corn starch. Gradually add hot milk or cream, stirring constantly. Set over low heat, bring to boiling, stirring steadily until desired thickness is reached. Sauce should be medium thick. Strain, serve warm.

Poppy seed loaf · Makový závin

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 8 tablespoons butter
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • dash of salt
  • 1 egg yolk to brush the loaf with
  • grease for baking sheet

Instructions

  1. Dissolve the yeast by mixing it with a teaspoon of sugar. Add three tablespoons of flour and 1/4 cup lukewarm milk; knead the leavening into a small ball. Let it rise 20 minutes, it should double in size.
  2. Place the remaining flour in a bowl, add eggs, lemon rind, remaining sugar, salt and milk, mix well. Finally add melted or softened butter. Knead the dough until it becames smooth and dry and no longer sticks to the bowl. Let it rise 30 minutes.
  3. Turn the dough onto a floured pastry board and roll it out into 1/8" thick rectangle. Cover evenly with poppy seed filling and roll it into a long narrow loaf (16-17" long). Place loaf on a greased baking sheet and let rise 10 minutes. Brush with lightly beaten egg yolk and bake 20-25 minutes in 35OF oven. When cooled, cut in 3/4" slices and serve.

Yield / time

1 loaf - Preparation time: 2 hours 30 minutes

Czech doughnuts · Koblihy

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 6 egg yolks
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar mixed with vanilla sugar
  • 1 tablespoon rum
  • 1 tablespoon orange juice
  • pinch of salt
  • 3/4 cup thick fruit preserve
  • 2 cups oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Leavening: In a bowl stir yeast with 2 teaspoons sugar until liquefied. Add 1 cup flour and 3/4 cup milk. Mix thoroughly. Knead into a smooth neat ball. Dust with flour, cover with a napkin and let rise 20 minutes. It should double in bulk.
  2. In a separate bowl cream egg yolks and vanilla sugar. Gradually adding heavy cream beat at low speed until foamy. Blend in remaining flour, salt, rum, orange juice and risen leavening. Mix thoroughly. Knead into a smooth, dry, elastic dough. Let rise 30 minutes.
  3. Punch dough down, turn onto a floured pastry board and roll out to 1/3" thickness.
  4. Using a cookie cutter or a small cup, cut out round cakes, about 2-1/2" in diameter.
  5. Drop fruit preserve by teaspoonfuls in center of half of round cakes. Top each with a plain round cake. Press borders firmly together. Be sure they are well sealed to prevent the filling from leaking out. With a smaller round cutter cut out the final shape of the doughnuts. Put doughnuts on lightly floured board, cover with a large napkin, let rest 20 minutes. Fry in hot oil, starting with top side down, in a covered frying pan. When bottom is golden brown turn them over and finish frying, uncovered. Drain on a sieve or paper towel. When cooled, sprinkle with sugar.

Yield / time

Serves 8 – Preparation time: 3 hours
