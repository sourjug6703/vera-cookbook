Venison cutlets, or medallions · Srnčí žebírka

Ingredients

  • 12 young venison cutlets, or medallions, 3 oz. ea.
  • 12 juniper berries, crushed
  • 4 tablespoons cognac
  • salt and pepper
  • 1·1/2 cups sour cream
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 3/4 cup olive oil
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 lb. fresh mushrooms, sliced lengthwise
  • snipped parsley

Instructions

  1. Trim excess fat from the cutlets, pound them lightly, rub with juniper berries, sprinkle with salt and pepper, drizzle with cognac and olive oil. Cover, marinate at least 1 hour.
  2. Meanwhile melt butter in a large skillet, sauté onion and mushrooms. Season with salt and pepper. Reserve.
  3. Drain and wipe the cutlets, reserve marinade. Heat remaining oil in a heavy frying pan, fry venison over high heat, about 3 minutes each side. Remove cutlets, keep them warm. Deglaze pan with marinade. Add chicken broth and sour cream, simmer 5 minutes. Taste for salt and pepper.
  4. Coat the cutlets, or medallions, with the sauce. Garnish with mushrooms, snipped parsley, poached halves of apples, serve with noodles, rice.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 20 minutes, plus marinating

Roast pheasant · Bažant pečený

Ingredients

  • 1 pheasant
  • 2 slices bacon cut in short strips
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3·4 tablespoons flour
  • 1·1/2 cups chicken broth
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Rinse and wipe the pheasant inside and out. Lard the breast and thighs with bacon strips. Brown the pheasant evenly all around in a frying pan in half of the butter and onion. Preheat oven to 350F. Transfer pheasant, breast down, into an ovenproof deep dish together with the butter and onion from the frying pan. Add salt and pepper. Roast 30·40 minutes, until the top gets reddish. Baste often with the remaining butter and chicken broth. Turn the bird breast side up, continue roasting in the same manner, 40·45 minutes. On the stove top bring the pan juices to boil. Add flour and cook 5 minutes stirring constantly. Add the remaining chicken broth and cook a little more. When the sauce thickens, strain it and pour over pheasant, cut in four portion sizes. Serve with red cabbage, potatoes, cranberries.

Yield / time

Serves 4 – Preparation time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Partridge/Quail · Koroptev/Křepelka

Ingredients

  • 6 quails
  • 3 slices bacon
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup chicken broth, or water
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Rinse quails, pat dry. Sprinkle with salt and pepper inside and outside. Place 1/2 slice bacon inside each. Heat butter in a flameproof casserole, add quails. Roast 40·50 minutes at 350F. Baste often with pan drippings, chicken broth or water. Serve dish straight from the oven with quails cut in halves. Garnish with fresh parsley sprigs, red cabbage, cranberry sauce, poached halves of apples. Serve with rice, lentils.

  • 1·1/2 cups water or fish broth
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons paprika
  • salt
  • 3 tablespoons butter and 4 tablespoons flour for the roux
  • Optional: 1/2 cup heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Melt butter in a large saucepot, add onion and cook until translucent. Add parsley and paprika, simmer 3 minutes. Add the fish and some of the fish broth or water.
  2. Cover and simmer 15·20 minutes.
  3. Using a slotted spatula, lift the carp portions onto a heated platter. For sauce, melt butter in a skillet and stir in flour. Cook and stir until golden.
  4. Add cooking liquid from the carp and some of the fish broth if needed.Cook until sauce thickens. Optional: for a creamier sauce blend in heavy cream. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Strain over fish. Serve with mashed potatoes, rice, tomato salad.

Baked pike with sour cream · Štika pečená se smetanou

Ingredients

  • 1 pike, 4 lbs., cleaned, with head and tail left on
  • 2 bacon slices cut in thin strips
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 4 medium potatoes, parboiled, sliced
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • salt
  • 1 pint sour cream

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion in butter.
  2. Meanwhile, sprinkle pike with salt, paprika and lemon juice. Lard with strips of bacon. Lay fish in a lightly greased baking pan. Arrange potato slices on sides of fish and up, towards the edge of the pan. Sprinkle some salt over potatoes.
  3. Pour the butter onion mixture over fish. Top with sour cream. Bake at 350F, 30·40 minutes.
  4. Serve with tomato salad, vegetables.

Salmon trout with dill sauce · Pstruh s koprovou omáčkou

Ingredients

  • 6 trouts, 4 lbs., cleaned, with heads and tails left on
  • 1/2 bottle white wine
  • 1·1/2 quarts water
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • juice of one lemon
  • 10 black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • parsley, finely chopped
  • salt
Sauce:
  • 3 tablespoons butter and 4 tablespoons flour
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped dill
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 cups fish broth
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Heat the wine, water, onion, lemon juice, bay leaves and peppercorns. Simmer 10 minutes. Add the trouts and simmer gently for 15 minutes, without boiling.
  2. Drain fish, arrange on a heated serving platter, keep it warm. Strain the broth from the fish, reserve 2 cups for dill sauce. Meanwhile, prepare the sauce. Melt butter in a saucepan, blend in flour, stir and cook until golden. Add onion, parsley, fish broth, vinegar, sugar, salt. Cook, uncovered, until sauce thickens. Strain. Blend in the cream and chopped dill. Heat through, do not boil. Pour hot butter over trouts, garnish with parsley. Hand sauce separately.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 45 minutes

Ham and noodles casserole · Šunkové nudle

Ingredients

  • 12 oz. egg noodles
  • 8 oz. fully cooked ham slices, cut into short strips
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • salt
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 tablespoons bread crumbs.
  • 2/3 cup heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Cook noodles in lightly salted water, drain and splash with cold water. In a bowl cream butter, egg yolks, lemon rind. Combine mixture with noodles and ham. Blend thoroughly. Fold in 3 stiffly beaten egg whites. Put into buttered and bread crumbs coated casserole. Pour the cream evenly over top and bake uncovered at 350F 35·40 minutes or until golden brown on top. Serve straight from oven, with green salad or tomato salad.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour 15 minutes
