Potato dumplings · Bramborové knedlíky

Ingredients

  • 5 medium potatoes
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup cream of wheat, regular, not precooked
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 2 whole eggs
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Peel potatoes, cook in salted water, drain. Mash them, add butter, eggs, salt. Mix thoroughly, blend in flour and cream of wheat. Knead until dough is smooth and dry.
  2. Pinch off bits of dough, roll into 2" balls. Cook in lightly salted boiling water 10-12 minutes. Serve immediately.
  3. Serve with roast goose, pork, cabbage, sauerkraut.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Potato salad · Bramborový salát

Ingredients

  • 5 medium potatoes, boiled, cooled, peeled and diced
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 5 slices ham cut in thin strips
  • 7 pickled cucumbers, peeled, diced, squeezed dry
  • 2 large green apples, Granny Smith, peeled, cored, diced
  • 2/3 cup Hellmann's mayonnaise mixed with 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 2 teaspoons vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons white wine Worcestershire sauce
  • white pepper

Instructions

  1. Place potatoes in a large bowl, sprinkle with vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, pepper. Add onions, ham, apples. Add pickles last, making sure they are squeezed dry. Lightly toss together all ingredients. With a spatula thoroughly but carefully blend in mayonnaise/mustard mixture. Chill to blend flavors, at least several hours. To serve, spoon salad in a bowl lined with lettuce. Serve with roasts, chicken, meatloaf.

Serves 8-10 – Preparation time 1 hour 15 minutes

Goulash soup · Gulášová polévka

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 quarts beef stock
  • 1 lb. beef stew meat cut in 1/2" cubes
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 slices bacon cut in short strips
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and cut in 1/2" cubes
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • 4 tablespoons flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon each of caraway seeds, marjoram, ground nutmeg
  • 3 cloves garlic, pressed or chopped
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. In a large saucepan combine oil, bacon and onion, brown it lightly. Add paprika, beef, pepper, caraway seeds, marjoram, nutmeg, garlic. Cover and simmer until beef is almost tender, about 45 minutes, stirring occasionally and adding 2-3 tablespoons beef stock, just enough to keep moisture in the pan. Uncover, blend in flour, braise 5 minutes. Add potatoes, beef stock, bring to boiling. Cook over medium/low heat until potatoes and beef are tender, 15-20 minutes. Season to taste with salt and pepper.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 2 hours

Cream of chicken soup · Krém z kuřat

Ingredients

  • 2 quarts water
  • 1 small chicken, 2 lbs., quartered
  • 2 beef soup bones
  • soup greens (carrot, celery, parsley)
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 1/3 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • salt and pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter and 3 tablespoons flour for the roux

Instructions

  1. Place water, chicken, bones, soup greens, onion, salt in a large saucepan. Bring to boiling. Simmer, covered, 45 minutes or until chicken is tender.
  2. Remove chicken when cool, discard skin and bones, puree chicken meat. Reserve.
  3. In a separate saucepan stir flour into melted butter and cook until bubbly and golden brown. Gradually add the broth from the chicken, bones, allspice and pepper. Cook 10 minutes over medium heat. Strain. Stir in puréed chicken. Lightly beat egg yolk and cream and blend mixture into soup. Season with salt and pepper. Heat through. Do not boil after egg yolk/cream addition.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 2 hours

Tripe soup · Drštková polévka

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs. tripe
  • 2 quarts chicken broth
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 celery root, or 4 stalks
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 4 cloves garlic, pressed or chopped
  • 2 tablespoons paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon each of marjoram, ground allspice, black pepper, ginger
  • 3 tablespoons butter and 4 tablespoons flour for the roux
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Most supermarkets and butchers sell tripe already cleaned and parboiled, so just rinse it well in cold water. However, if you need to start from scratch, here's how:
  2. Clean tripe well and rinse thoroughly under running cold water. Rub with salt, scald with boiling water, then rinse again under running cold water. Put in a large pot, cover with salted water, and boil covered for 1 hour. Drain. Rinse thoroughly in cold water again. Start with clean salted water and boil tripe until almost tender. 1-2 hours.
  3. Cook tripe with carrots, celery and onion in chicken broth until tripe
  4. and vegetables are tender, 30-40 minutes. Remove tripe and vegetables, reserve the broth.
  5. Melt butter in a saucepan. Stir in flour to make a smooth paste. Cook and stir until golden. Add garlic, paprika, marjoram, allspice, pepper and ginger. Brown mixture lightly. Add the broth, simmer 10 minutes, strain.
  6. Cut tripe in 2" long thin strips, slice vegetables, stir into soup. Season to taste with salt and pepper.

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Country potato soup · Bramborová polévka

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 quarts water
  • 3 medium potatoes, peeled, cut in cubes
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, pressed
  • 1/2 teaspoon each of caraway seeds, and marjoram
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 1 cup fresh mushrooms, sliced lengthwise, sautéed in butter
  • parsley, finely chopped
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. In a large saucepot combine potatoes, salt, caraway seeds, marjoram, water. Cook 20 minutes, or until potatoes are crisp-tender.
  2. Sauté onion and garlic in butter, stir in flour, brown lightly. Add 2 cups cooking liquid from the potatoes. Bring to boiling, cook and stir 5 minutes. Strain mixture into potatoes. Simmer 10 minutes, add mushrooms and parsley. Season to taste with salt and pepper.

Bohemian open-faced pastries · České koláčky

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 2 egg yolks
  • dash of grated nutmeg
  • 1 whole egg for brushing
  • 1 cup filling or thick fruit preserve

Instructions

  1. Leavening: In a bowl stir yeast with 2 teaspoons sugar until dissolved. Add 2/3 cup flour and 1/2 cup lukewarm milk. Knead into a ball. Dust with flour, cover with napkin, let rise in a warm place. It will double in bulk within 20 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl combine remaining flour and sugar, lemon rind, nutmeg, salt. Place risen leavening in center.
  3. Lightly beat egg yolks and the remaining lukewarm milk, then add it gradually to flour/leavening while mixing thoroughly. Finally add melted lukewarm butter and knead dough until smooth and elastic.
  4. Dust with flour, cover with a napkin and let rise 40 minutes in a warm place.
  5. Punch dough down and turn onto lightly floured pastry board. Break off dough and form balls the size of a walnut. Place them on a greased baking sheet, 4" apart. Let rise 10 minutes.
  6. Press down the center of each ball, using bottom of a small glass or other round object, or with your fingers, so that a higher border will be formed. Brush borders with beaten egg. Spread filling evenly over center wells. Let rise 15 minutes.
  7. (If using butter crumbs filling, brush with melted butter first. This way filling will stick).
  8. Preheat oven to 350F, bake 15 minutes, or until borders are golden brown.
  9. Dust with sugar while warm.

Yield / time

Serves 10 – Preparation time: 3 hours

Bohemian muffins · Buchty

Ingredients

  • 4 cups coarse flour, Gold Medal Wondra
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 1-1/4 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 whole egg
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 8 tablespoons butter
  • dash of salt
  • 4 tablespoons butter for brushing and for baking pan
  • 1 cup filling

Instructions

  1. Leavening: In a bowl stir yeast with 2 teaspoons sugar until dissolved. Add 2/3 cup flour and 1/2 cup lukewarm milk. Knead into a ball. Dust with flour, cover with a napkin, set aside to rise – in a warm draft free place. It should double in bulk within 15-20 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl blend remaining flour with sugar, lemon rind and salt. Push to sides making a well in the center. Place risen leavening in the well.
  3. Lightly beat egg yolks and whole egg in remaining lukewarm milk. Add it gradually to flour/leavening mixture, mixing thoroughly. Lastly, add melted lukewarm butter. Knead dough until smooth and no longer sticks to the bowl. Dust with flour, cover with napkin and let it rise 40 minutes.
  4. Turn dough over onto a floured pastry board and roll it out to 1/3" thickness. Cut into 2 × 3" rectangles. Drop filling by teaspoonfuls on narrower side of each rectangle, roll over so that filling will be well covered with the dough. Pinch sides firmly to seal them. Dip each piece in butter, or brush on all sides with melted butter. Arrange them close together in a buttered baking pan. Let them rise 30 minutes. Preheat oven to 350F and bake 25 minutes or until golden on top. Invert 'buchty' onto a wire rack and cool, at least 20 minutes. Separate them carefully and sprinkle with sugar.

Yield / time

Serves 10 – Preparation time: 3 hours
