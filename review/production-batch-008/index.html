Ham and noodles casserole · Šunkové nudle

Ingredients

  • 12 oz. egg noodles
  • 8 oz. fully cooked ham slices, cut into short strips
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • salt
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 tablespoons bread crumbs.
  • 2/3 cup heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Cook noodles in lightly salted water, drain and splash with cold water. In a bowl cream butter, egg yolks, lemon rind. Combine mixture with noodles and ham. Blend thoroughly. Fold in 3 stiffly beaten egg whites. Put into buttered and bread crumbs coated casserole. Pour the cream evenly over top and bake uncovered at 350F 35·40 minutes or until golden brown on top. Serve straight from oven, with green salad or tomato salad.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour 15 minutes

Stuffed peppers · Plněné papriky

Ingredients

  • 6 medium size green peppers
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 lb. finely ground pork or beef
  • 1 egg, whole
  • 1/2 cup firmly cooked rice
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Slice stem ends from peppers, remove white fiber and seeds. Drop into boiling salted water, simmer 1 minute. Drain on a sieve or paper towel. Pat dry cavities.
  2. Meanwhile, heat oil in a skillet, add onion and ground meat, sauté 10 minutes, mixing often. Blend in the egg, rice and salt. Spoon mixture into peppers, pressing firmly towards bottom and sides.
  3. Arrange stuffed peppers in a flameproof buttered casserole, pour tomato sauce over all. Simmer, covered, 30·35 minutes. Serve with noodles or gnocchi.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour 15 minutes

Stuffed cabbage rolls · Plněná kapusta

Ingredients

  • 1 savoy cabbage head
  • 1/2 lb. ground veal
  • 1/2 lb. ground pork
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 hard baked roll
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 small onions, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, pressed or chopped
  • 1 egg, whole
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • minced parsely
  • marjoram
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Pull off 12 large leaves from the cabbage head. Parboil them in salted water, place each on a napkin and flatten it with rolling pin or hand. Soak roll in milk, squeeze off excess and shred it. In a mixing bowl combine ground meat, shredded roll, 1 chopped onion, minced parsely, marjoram, garlic, salt and pepper. Mix thoroughly and spread evenly on the cabbage leaves. Roll them tightly, fasten each with a string or wooden pick. In a large skillet sauté onion in butter, add the stuffed cabbage rolls, simmer 30·40 minutes, adding occasionally some of the chicken stock. Lift rolls onto a serving dish, sprinkle with juices from the pan, serve with rice or potatoes.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour 15 minutes

Green cabbage · Hlávkové zelí

Ingredients

  • 1 head of cabbage, about 2 lbs.
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 4 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • caraway seeds
  • 1/3 cup vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Discard outside leaves from the fresh head of cabbage. Quarter, core, rinse and cut in strips. Scald with boiling water. Drain. Sauté onion in oil until translucent. Add cabbage, salt and caraway seeds. Simmer, turning occasionally, until cabbage is tender but crisp, about 15·20 minutes. Blend in flour, simmer 2·3 minutes. Add vinegar and sugar, simmer 1·2 minutes until thoroughly heated through. Serve with pork roast, meatloaf, smoked pork.

Red cabbage · Červené zelí

Ingredients

  • 1 fresh head of red cabbage, about 2 lbs.
  • 1·1/2 cups vinegar
  • 4 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 oz. bacon, diced
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • caraway seeds
  • 1 large apple, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 2/3 cup red wine
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Remove wilted outside leaves from the head of red cabbage. Quarter, core and rinse it. Cut in strips, scald with hot vinegar. In a skillet combine oil, bacon, onion, caraway seeds and sugar. Sauté mixture until brownned. Add cabbage, thinly sliced apple, dash of salt, cover and simmer 15 minutes, or until cabbage is almost tender. Uncover, let liquid almost dry out, blend in flour and red wine, simmer 5·10 minutes. Cabbage should be tender but crisp. Serve with roast goose, duckling, pork, game.

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 45 minutes

Sauerkraut · Kyselé zelí

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs. sauerkraut, drained
  • 4 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • caraway seeds
  • salt

Instructions

Gnocchi · Krupicové noky

Ingredients

  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 2 whole eggs
  • 7 oz. regular cream of wheat, not precooked
  • salt

Instructions

  1. In a bowl cream butter and eggs, add cream of wheat, salt, mix thoroughly. Let it rest 20 minutes.
  2. Using a wet teaspoon, scoop gnocchi into lightly salted boiling water. Cook over medium heat for 10 minutes, uncovered. Remove pan from heat, cover and let stand 5 minutes. Drain.

Country gnocchi · Škubánky

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs. potatoes, peeled, rinsed, quartered
  • 1·1/2 cups flour
  • 5 tablespoons butter
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Parboil potatoes in lightly salted water. Drain some of the water, leaving potatoes barely covered. Stir in flour and simmer over low heat about 10 minutes or until potatoes flake when tested with a fork. Drain excess water, if any, salt the mixture and mash it thoroughly. Mixture will be rather thick.
  2. Meanwhile, brown onion in butter.
  3. Dip a tablespoon in hot butter and scoop gnocchi onto a warmed serving dish. Sprinkle with browned onion and butter. Serve as a side dish with meats, or as a meal.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour

Notes

  • Alternative – Substitute ground poppy seeds mixed with powdered sugar for browned onion. Sprinkle with hot butter.

Dumplings with croutons · Houskové knedlíky

Ingredients

  • 6 cups coarse flour, (Gold Medal Wondra)
  • 1 whole egg
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2·1/2 cups club soda
  • 3 cups croutons, (4 hard baked rolls)
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • salt

Instructions

  1. Croutons: Cut rolls in 1/2" thick slices, cut each slice in uniform squares, braise lightly in butter. Set aside.
  2. In a bowl combine flour, egg, egg yolk, salt, and club soda. Knead until the dough is smooth and no longer sticks to the bowl. Dough should be of medium consistency, not too thick. Add croutons, mix well. Turn the dough over onto a floured pastry board and form two oblong balls-dumplings. Place them in lightly salted boiling water and boil over medium heat for 25 minutes. While cooking turn them with a spatula.
  3. Remove dumplings to a cutting board and cut each into 1/2 · 3/4" slices. Use a thread or very sharp knife. Brush each slice lightly with melted butter.
  4. Serve with roasts, fowl, game, sauces.
  5. Dumpling slices can be frozen and reheated in double boiler.

Yield / time

Serves 6 - Preparation time: 45 minutes

Yeast dumplings · Kynuté knedlíky

Ingredients

  • 5 cups coarse flour, Gold Medal Wondra
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1·1/2 cups milk, lukewarm
  • grated rind of 1 lemon
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1 oz. fresh yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • salt
Optional:
  • 3 tablespoons fried bread crumbs

Instructions

  1. Leavening: In a bowl stir fresh yeast and sugar until it dissolves. Add 1/4 cup lukewarm milk and 1/3 cup flour, mix thoroughly into a smooth ball. Dust with flour, set aside to rise, in a warm draft free place. Should double in bulk within 15 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl combine remaining flour, baking powder, lemon rind, salt. Make a well in center where you put egg yolks and the risen leavening. Mix all, gradually adding the remaining lukewarm milk. Knead until dough is smooth and separates from the bowl. Let it rise in a warm place for 30 minutes.
  3. Cut tablespoonsfuls of dough, round them into balls, let them rise on a floured surface for 10 minutes. Drop dumplings into rapidly boiling salted water, boil 5 minutes, turn them around with a spatula and cook another 5 minutes. Drain, pierce each with fork, sprinkle with hot butter.
  4. Optional: Coat with fried bread crumbs. Serve with meats and sauces.

Yield / time

Serves 6 – Preparation time: 1 hour 15 minutes
